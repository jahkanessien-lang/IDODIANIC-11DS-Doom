# 11DS Threat Model

## Assets

Identity, data, devices, applications, infrastructure, networks, operational telemetry, physical systems, AI systems, backups/recovery systems, and governance evidence.

## Representative threats

- Credential theft
- Privilege escalation
- Endpoint compromise
- Supply-chain compromise
- Cloud/control-plane abuse
- Lateral movement
- Data tampering or exfiltration
- Insider misuse
- AI-agent overreach
- Ransomware and destructive events
- Loss of power/connectivity
- Recovery corruption

## Defensive response

Use layered prevention, least privilege, segmentation, monitoring, containment, evidence preservation, trusted recovery, and independent assurance.
